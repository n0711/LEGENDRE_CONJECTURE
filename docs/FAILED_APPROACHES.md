# Failed Approaches

## F0: Naive RH gap route

**FAILED AS A DIRECT PROOF.** The RH explicit formula gives only
`O(sqrt(x) log^2 x)` or a classical gap consequence of
`O(sqrt(x) log x)`.  The gap required for Legendre is `O(sqrt{x})` in each
square interval.  Removing the logarithmic factor is exactly the missing
obstruction, and RH alone is not known to supply it directly.
